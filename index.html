A little about me

Reliable execution, with an eye for the details.

Good operations are not always about doing more. They are about making sure the right work gets done accurately, consistently, and without unnecessary confusion.

My background combines publishing support, content operations, data validation, quality checks, documentation, and business development support.

In comparison-site publishing, I worked with mobile, broadband, and energy content, checking provider information and updating website data to help keep offers, prices, and plan details accurate and current.

I have also supported email-based business development, prospect research, LinkedIn outreach, and lead-generation activities. I enjoy work that requires organisation, follow-through, clear communication, and attention to detail.

Today, I want to bring that experience to founders and remote teams that need dependable support with the recurring work behind their business.
